- **Terminal sale — Thread A resolved 8/9/2026.** 2026 pattern that started this thread: multiple families repurchased their goats and sheep from the floor buyers after the sale; animals resurfaced on social-media sale pages roughly six months later; the effect was double-dipping — sale check, animal back, private resale. The premium buyer base, the fair's high-priced and deeply supportive buyers, was angered and soured by it. No names recorded, by design. Full decision surface (TA-1 through TA-4, R3, R3b, SALE-01, O1a through O4, N1 through N4) locked and drafted this session; see delta-log SALE-LIMITS-01 (adopted) and SALE-TERMINAL-01, SALE-01 (both DISCUSS-AT-WALKTHROUGH, full proposed text in the log). Mechanism: the current book licensed the leak by assigning trucking, slaughter, and processing to the exhibitor; the proposal closes it by custody rather than prohibition, following CSF's own architecture, with an explicit product-not-live-animal clause added since CSF's own book leaves that implicit. This capture is the incident evidence for the board conversation, stated opponent-proof: behavior pattern and buyer-relations stake, no blame on any named person.
- **Beef post-sale rest custom (8/9/2026, veteran knowledge, newly captured).** Long-standing practice, never previously written down anywhere: sale steers were commonly taken home for about a week to calm down and restore muscle glycogen before processing, since a stressed slaughter animal risks dark-cutting, docked meat. The terminal-sale custody chain (SALE-TERMINAL-01) can't accommodate an at-home rest week, so the practice's underlying purpose, meat quality, is preserved a different way: a purchaser-requested rest period is arranged by the Authority inside the designated-facility chain (floor buyers already hold cattle in their own yards as normal business), with holding cost withheld from proceeds like any other delivery cost. Mechanism goes to the June RFP cycle; see the floor-buyer brief.
