- **Beef chute — market sale day safety.** Spectators entering the chute during the 2026 sale was a real safety issue. Two-part fix: (1) physical barrier (railing, panels, or fencing), (2) safety reminder in the announcer script before the sale and repeated during. *(added 7/23/2026)*

Layout optimization is separate homework, not a board action item. *(added 7/23/2026)*
