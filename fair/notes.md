- **Beef post-sale rest custom (8/9/2026, veteran knowledge, newly captured).** Long-standing practice, never previously written down anywhere: sale steers were commonly taken home for about a week to calm down and restore muscle glycogen before processing, since a stressed slaughter animal risks dark-cutting, docked meat. The terminal-sale custody chain (SALE-TERMINAL-01) can't accommodate an at-home rest week, so the practice's underlying purpose, meat quality, is preserved a different way: a purchaser-requested rest period is arranged by the Authority inside the designated-facility chain (floor buyers already hold cattle in their own yards as normal business), with holding cost withheld from proceeds like any other delivery cost. Mechanism goes to the June RFP cycle; see the floor-buyer brief.
