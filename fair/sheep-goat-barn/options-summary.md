# Sheep/Goat Barn — 5 Layout Options
## Response to `optimization-brief.md` — Custer County Fair

Five distinct static layouts for the 20 pens (4'x6' nominal, 4'3"x6'3" outside) and 4 wire cages (6'x6' nominal, 6'3"x6'3" outside), each built to absorb all 8 phases without moving penning mid-fair. All diagrams are to scale, wall thickness accounted, colorblind-safe (no red/green as a sole meaning-carrier anywhere).

Image files referenced below are delivered alongside this summary — see `README.md` in this folder for the upload list.

---

## Option A — Corrected Perimeter + Center Spine

![Option A layout](./option_A_layout.png)
![Option A phases](./option_A_phases.png)

A north-wall row plus one back-to-back center spine. Two oversized aisles (9-10.5') do double duty as weigh-in queuing and swine-holding overflow. This is the most conservative option: closest in spirit to the current arrangement, but corrected — cages cleared off the electrical panel, aisles verified at 4' minimum throughout, gates hinged to swing against a blocking surface.

- Proposed +4 pens install as a fifth row in the big center aisle, snug against the spine.
- Expansion ceiling: **+6 pens** beyond the proposed 4, using the remaining aisle width.
- Fitting-stand zone doubles as swine holding (dual-use, not permanent) per the brief's baseline expectation.

## Option B — Back-to-Back Double Rows

![Option B layout](./option_B_layout.png)
![Option B phases](./option_B_phases.png)

Four long rows in two back-to-back pairs, stacked north to south. Highest pen density of the five — every pen shares a straight-line aisle with no wasted circulation.

- Proposed +4 pens slot into the pair-1/pair-2 aisle, flush against the lower pair.
- Expansion ceiling: **+4 pens** — the tightest of the five; the design's density is also its ceiling.
- Trade-off: rows run the full 31' width, so the walk from the west end of any row to the garage door is the longest of the five options.

## Option C — U-Shape / Horseshoe (open to the show ring)

![Option C layout](./option_C_layout.png)
![Option C phases](./option_C_phases.png)

Pens ring the north wall, the interior-west leg, and the south-east floor; the horseshoe's mouth opens east, directly at the garage door / show ring. Every phase's traffic — weigh-in, swine holding, sheep/goat show, auction — funnels through one open core with a straight sightline out.

- Proposed +4 pens sit flush atop the top-inner leg, snug below the top-outer leg (tightest install of the five — noted as a real trade-off, not hidden).
- Expansion ceiling: **+3 pens** — the lowest of the five. The open core that makes this option's flow work is the same floor space that limits growth.
- Best animal-flow score in the matrix; worst space-efficiency score. A genuine trade, not a flaw.

## Option D — Pod Clusters (4 quadrants)

![Option D layout](./option_D_layout.png)
![Option D phases](./option_D_phases.png)

Four pod groupings (a north row, two center-facing double rows, and a southwest cluster) leave the entire SE quadrant permanently clear — a real 9'9"x13' dedicated fitting/holding zone, not a dual-use footprint. This is the only option that clears the brief's Section 7 stretch goal outright.

- Proposed +4 pens slot into the aisle between the north row and the center pods.
- Expansion ceiling: **+4 pens**, in the pocket east of the proposed row.
- Highest Phase Adaptability score in the matrix, specifically because Phase 3 setup never touches the fitting stands at all.

## Option E — Angled Funnel to the Show Ring

![Option E layout](./option_E_layout.png)
![Option E phases](./option_E_phases.png)

Four short rows angled 6-9 degrees off the wall grid, converging toward the garage door like a funnel. The geometry itself — not just the open floor — shortens the walk from every pen to the show ring on both show days.

- Proposed +4 pens sit as a straight row in the center aisle (drawn axis-aligned to keep the swine-holding footprint simple to build).
- Expansion ceiling: **+5 pens** in the residual pocket north of the proposed row.
- Highest combined Movement + Animal Flow score in the matrix. Angling costs a small amount of floor at each row's trapezoidal ends — visible in the layout diagram, priced into the Space score.

## Comparison Matrix

![Comparison matrix](./comparison_matrix.png)

Every axis is scored independently, 1 (weakest) to 5 (strongest); the /20 total is a discussion aid, not an automatic winner — a low-Space/high-Flow option and a high-Space/low-Flow option can serve very different priorities depending on what matters most for fair week.

## Shared design notes (apply to all five)

- Electrical panel clearance (north wall, 5' from NW corner, 30"x36" floor-to-ceiling) is empty in every option — no cage ever sits in or in front of it; pens may sit adjacent, never inside.
- Every pen and cage footprint is drawn to outside dimensions (nominal + 3" per axis) — never centerlines.
- Gates hinge at aisle cross-partitions in every option, so an open gate always lands flat against a wall or a neighboring pen face — never into clear aisle.
- Aisles meet or exceed the 4' minimum throughout; the handful of intentionally tight installs (noted per option above) are the proposed-pen and expansion-ceiling overlays, not the current 20-pen/4-cage baseline.
- Swamp cooler and small scale each get one placement per option, chosen to serve Phase 1 weigh-in and stay clear for the rest of fair week — neither option relies on moving them mid-fair.
