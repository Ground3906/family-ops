## 1. Introduction / Authority & Scope
- Authority "reserves to itself the **final and absolute right** to interpret these competition requirements, to fairly and impartially settle and determine all matters, questions, and differences ... and the right to amend or add to these competition requirements as its judgments may determine." Also reserves right to cancel events or change scheduling "if necessary."
- Applies to "all divisions and all competitions" at CSF.
- "Management" = Authority + General Manager, Section Managers, Department Directors, Show Superintendents, Lead Clerks — "vested with the **discretionary power** to interpret rules, enforce safety and grooming standards, allocate physical resources, and adjudicate disputes."
- Entry submission binds exhibitor to: (a) entire Exhibitor Handbook, (b) Colorado State Fair Authority Rules "adopted at **8 CCR 1208-1**," and (c) the Exhibitor Code of Conduct, which "each exhibitor must sign."
- Competition "open to the world except where otherwise specified." No guarantee of competition; Authority may deny grounds access for symptoms of "potentially communicable illness" or refusal to comply with public health requirements — exhibitor must indemnify Authority/State of Colorado/Board/CO Dept. of Agriculture for such denial.
- Authority "is not responsible for any loss, damage, or injury to property while on its grounds."

## 2. Entries and Entry Forms
- All entries: regulation forms or online system, "properly filled out," accompanied by fees "whenever required."
- "No entry will be entitled to a place on the grounds without fully completing the entry process."
- "The Authority assumes no responsibility for entry forms or online entry system errors."
- **Entry Fees are non-refundable.** (stated flatly, no exception carve-out anywhere in document)
- Completed entry form = exhibitor's binding acceptance of all requirements for the full duration of the exhibitor's time at the Fair.

## 3. Exhibitor Admission (fees)
- Every exhibitor must purchase one of: All-Age Wristband, Youth Exhibitor Wristband, or Daily Gate Admission.
  - **All-Age Wristband: $45** — valid the full 11 days of the Fair; open to any age.
  - **Youth Exhibitor Wristband: $15** — discounted; only for youth actually entered in a 4-H or FFA show (non-competing 4-H/FFA family members do not qualify).
  - Daily General Admission — variable price, Gates 3/5/9, purchasable online.
- Authority may limit entries to facility capacity; may cancel a class/competition/division for insufficient entries (fees returned if canceled).
- Authority "may disqualify and remove any entry" entered in violation of requirements.
- Entries "may not be removed" before time set in division rules; no release without a properly executed release form (from Program Manager).
- No discrimination "based on race, creed, color, sex, age, or other non-merit factors."

## 4. Disputes (general clause)
- Authority "reserves the final and absolute right to interpret all of the Authority's competition requirements and Authority Rules and to settle interpretation questions."

## 5. Determination of Violations
- Violation determination made by **General Manager, Director of Agriculture and Competitive Exhibits ("Director"), or Program Manager** of the relevant division — can occur "before, during, or after judging."
- **Hard rule:** "At no time will any exhibition, judging event, or other ongoing event be delayed, stopped, or interrupted due to any allegation of wrongdoing" under investigation.
- Program Manager, in consultation with Director/General Manager, "shall" set penalty once a violation is found. Penalties (non-exclusive list):
  a. Disqualification + forfeiture of all entry/other fees + all premiums/trophies/awards from the division.
  b. Withdrawal/return of any/all premiums, trophies, awards won in **any and all** divisions; forfeiture of all entry/other fees.
  c. Bar from State Fair competition for a determined period, **"including a lifetime suspension,"** requiring concurrence of GM + Director + Program Manager.
  d. Entry in question barred from sale at any State Fair sale.
  e. For livestock already sold: Program Manager (w/ Director & GM) may allow exhibitor to keep sale proceeds minus costs "attributable to the exhibitor" (no defined formula/standard). If no proceeds are paid to exhibitor, Authority contacts buyer with 3 options: (1) full refund, (2) donate to Colorado State Fair Foundation, or (3) let Authority retain funds as deposit toward future years' sales.
  f. Catch-all: "The Program Manager, Director, or the General Manager **may impose any other appropriate penalty**" (undefined, open-ended).

## 6. Rights of Appeal and Process of Appeal (all deadlines are 15 days)
- **A.** Within **15 days** of a violation determination, General Manager must notify exhibitor in writing (mailed to entry-form address, or hand-delivered during the Fair) with facts supporting the violation.
- **B.** Notice must include description of any penalty assessed.
- **C.** Within **15 days of receipt** of that notice, exhibitor may appeal in writing to the Board; written appeal must reach the General Manager's office within 15 days.
- **D.** Appeal must include supporting facts.
- **E.** Within **15 days** of receiving the appeal, Program Manager/Director/GM **may rescind** the initial determination — if rescinded, no further determination may be made and **"the exhibitor will have no further remedy available"** (rescission is final/unreviewable).
- **F.** If no action taken within 15 days of appeal receipt, the appeal must go to the next regular Board meeting **solely to set a hearing date**; exhibitor notified in writing after that meeting.
- **G.** Board appoints a **4-person hearing panel**: (a) Board member as presiding officer, (b) an individual with expertise in the particular competition, (c) a member of the public, (d) an Authority senior employee other than the GM.
- **H.** Within **15 days** of the hearing's conclusion, presiding officer submits findings + recommended final determination (plus a copy of the exhibitor's appeal) to Board and exhibitor.
- **I.** At the next regular Board meeting, Board adopts a final determination; exhibitor receives a copy.

## 7. Grievances / Protests
- Any person may report alleged rule violations or "unethical activity" to the Program Manager (or designee) using a form available from the GM's or Program Manager's office.
- **Bond: $300**, required with every grievance submission; **refundable** if the reviewing official (GM/Director/Program Manager) determines the facts constitute a violation.
- **Deadline: within 24 hours** of the alleged infraction for **all livestock activities**; before the **official closing of the State Fair** for all other activities. If filed after judging on the item has begun, judging proceeds and the grievance is handled as if filed post-judging.
- Must be **written and sworn**, and must include: grievant's name/contact info, accused exhibitor's name, entry/class in question, specific allegations, supporting facts, and the specific rule/requirement provisions allegedly violated.
- Within **15 days** of receipt, the Director (or designee) must notify the grievant in writing of the validity determination and any resulting action; denials must state the reason(s).
- If deemed valid, Program Manager + Director appoint a **committee of three disinterested, qualified persons** to review and decide — that decision "shall be considered **final for purposes of appeal**" under these requirements.
- Grievant retains right to appeal per the same Rights-of-Appeal process (Section 6 above).

## 8. Exhibitor Liability / Barred Exhibitors / Entry Errors
- Exhibitor solely responsible for loss/injury/damage from their animal or exhibited article; must indemnify Authority/State/Board/CO Dept. of Agriculture broadly.
- **Barred Exhibitors:** Authority + Board, working with the **North American Livestock Show and Rodeo Managers Association (NALS&RMA)** and its **Rules & Infraction Database**, may refuse entry to anyone barred from another show for unethical practices, disqualified at a "major livestock show" or any state/county fair, or who had premiums withheld/withdrawn for a rule violation elsewhere.
- Entry Errors: "Authority accepts entries as presented and does not check those entries for errors" — exhibitor is responsible for all entry-form errors.

## 9. Money-Handling Rules
**Returned Checks:**
- **$35 service charge** for any check returned insufficient/uncleared.
- **5 working days** to repay (cash or money order) the check amount plus the $35 charge.
- "**No exhibitor will be permitted to show** until the insufficient check has been paid."
- If check is returned after exhibitor has already shown, **all premiums held** until paid (amount + $35).
- Non-reinstatement within **60 days** → referred to a state-authorized collections agency.

**IRS / CO Dept. of Revenue Compliance:**
- Current **IRS Form W-9** required for any premium earnings of **$600 or more**, or any sale-derived monies.
- "No monies won or earned ... will be paid if the IRS Form W-9 is incorrect."

**Premium Checks/Refunds:**
- Issued only to the individual exhibitor whose name/SSN match the accepted W-9. **"Checks will not be issued to an exhibitor's relative, company, or fitter."**
- Lost check → notify Program Manager immediately.
- Must be cashed **within six months** of issue date.
- Exhibitors have **until December 31** of the competition year to claim checks returned to the Authority; uncashed checks after that are void.
- **"No replacement checks will be issued after December 31"** of the current year.

**Special/Sponsored Awards:** collected directly from contributing business/individual — Authority not responsible for redemption.

## 10. Premium Awards / Judging-Adjacent Provisions
*(Note: no Danish/American ribbon-system methodology, placing criteria, or class-construction rules appear anywhere in this document — this section is the entirety of what the doc says about awards/judging.)*
- "Awards and ribbons **have no cash value**."
- "Premiums will be paid only from the records of the judges' sheets."
- Premium checks issued "as soon as auditing, drug testing, if applicable, and processing can be completed" — no drug-testing procedure, panel, or threshold specified anywhere in doc.
- "Sponsored awards may be added or changed."

## 11. Bedding / Camping
- Exhibitors must follow bedding rules set in each program's own specific competition requirements (none specified here).
- On-grounds camping limited to **pre-paid reserved spots only**; no camping/overnight stays in Authority parking lots or barn areas.

## 12. Restrictions and Limitations
- **Participation restriction:** Board members, full-time staff, and their immediate family are prohibited from exhibiting/competing in any category/event where they are directly or indirectly involved in judging; must disclose conflicts of interest and may need to recuse (to the Program Manager).
- **"No volunteer within a State Fair division may act as a judge in any competition within such division."** (absolute)
- **Interference prohibition:** Direct criticism/interference with a judge, Authority staff (incl. temporary/part-time/contractors), other exhibitors, association reps, or show officials — before, during, or after the event — is prohibited. "**No person shall direct abusive or threatening conduct**" toward them. Violation → Program Manager/Director/GM **may**: remove exhibitor from judging area, disqualify from further competition, remove from fairgrounds, withhold prizes from any other Fair event, and/or recommend suspension from future State Fairs **"for a period of up to three (3) years."**
- **Dogs:** none allowed on grounds except leashed competition dogs or service animals (citing **Authority Rules §6.2, 8 CCR 1208-1**).
- **Motorized vehicles** (golf carts, motorcycles, mopeds, hoverboards, scooters): require **pre-approval by the General Manager**, based on GM's judgment of the applicant's demonstrated "**hardship**" without one (no objective standard defined).
- **Non-motorized vehicles** (bicycles, skateboards, scooters): **"are not allowed at any time"** — flat prohibition, no exception/approval pathway offered (contrast with motorized vehicles, which do have a GM waiver route).

## 13. Food, Advertising, and Livestock Hygiene
- All exhibits/concessions/commercial privileges require Authority approval. "**Under no circumstances** will any advertising matter be allowed" on buildings/trees/grounds or distributed "without the General Manager's permission" (i.e., absolute except for GM sign-off).
- Livestock hygiene is phrased as **advisory, not mandatory**: "we **advise** all participants to refrain from eating in the livestock barns and to thoroughly wash his or her hands ..." — this is the one hygiene-adjacent provision in the doc, and it deliberately uses "advise," not "must/shall."
- Food distribution/sale restricted to those with a "fully executed vendor agreement"; Authority may confiscate unauthorized food/materials and impose discipline per the Determination of Violations section.

## LANGUAGE PATTERN ANALYSIS

**Modal-verb frequency (whole-document, exact counts via full-text search):**
| Term | Count | Typical use |
|---|---|---|
| "must" | 24 | Binds **exhibitors** to concrete obligations (complete entry process, purchase wristband, sign Code of Conduct, submit W-9, meet appeal/grievance deadlines, indemnify Authority) |
| "may" | 35 | Overwhelmingly grants discretion to **Authority actors** (GM/Director/Program Manager/Board) — to disqualify, cancel, rescind, penalize, refuse entry, approve vehicles, add awards, etc. Rarely grants exhibitors discretion. |
| "shall" | 8 | Used almost exclusively as a **procedural duty imposed on officials/panel members**, not on exhibitors: Program Manager "shall" set penalty; hearing-panel Board member "shall serve" as presiding officer; presiding officer "shall submit" findings; Director "shall notify" grievant; committee decision "shall be considered final." The one exhibitor-facing "shall" is negative/prohibitive: "No person **shall** direct abusive or threatening conduct." |
| "should" | 1 | **Not used in the normal advisory/recommended sense at all.** The sole instance ("**Should** the Program Manager, Director, or General Manager take no action on the appeal within 15 days...") is a conditional/"if" construction, not a softer obligation level. The document has essentially no soft-recommendation register — it is binary must/shall vs. may. |
| "discretion(ary)" | 1 explicit ("Management is vested with the **discretionary power** to interpret rules...") | but discretion is functionally pervasive via "may" and via undefined standards (see below) |

**Discretion / escape-hatch clauses reserved for staff or judges:**
- Blanket interpretive authority: Authority's "**final and absolute right** to interpret" all requirements (stated twice — Intro §1 and the standalone "Disputes" section).
- Open-ended penalty catch-all: "...**may impose any other appropriate penalty**" — no boundaries given.
- Undefined-standard discretion: sale-proceeds deductions "**representative of any costs attributable to the exhibitor**" — amount set by Program Manager/Director/GM with no formula.
- Unreviewable discretion: officials "**may rescind**" a violation determination during appeal, and if they do, "the exhibitor will have **no further remedy available**" — i.e., discretion exercised in the exhibitor's favor is final and can't be re-opened by anyone.
- Subjective-threshold discretion: motorized-vehicle pre-approval turns on the GM's judgment of "**hardship**," undefined.
- Conflict-of-interest self-policing: Board/staff/volunteers "**may need to recuse**" themselves — self-assessed, reported "to the respective Program Manager."

**Explicit "no exception" / hard-and-fast rules (absolute language, no carve-out offered in-document):**
- "Entry Fees are **non-refundable**." (no exceptions listed anywhere)
- "**No entry** will be entitled to a place on the grounds without fully completing the entry process."
- "**At no time** will any exhibition, judging event, or other ongoing event be delayed, stopped, or interrupted" for a pending wrongdoing allegation.
- "**No volunteer** within a State Fair division may act as a judge in any competition within such division."
- Non-motorized vehicles "**are not allowed at any time**" (contrast: motorized vehicles *do* have a defined GM-waiver path — the drafters clearly distinguish "absolute-absolute" bans from "absolute-unless-approved" bans).
- "**No exhibitor** will be permitted to show until the insufficient check has been paid."
- "Checks **will not be** issued to an exhibitor's relative, company, or fitter."
- "**No monies** won or earned ... will be paid if the IRS Form W-9 is incorrect."
- "**No replacement checks** will be issued after December 31."
- "**Under no circumstances**" will unapproved advertising be allowed (exception valve: GM permission only).
- "**No person shall** direct abusive or threatening conduct" toward judges/staff — the prohibition itself is unconditional even though the resulting penalty is discretionary ("may").

**Overall pattern:** Obligations flow down (exhibitor "must"/"shall not"); discretion flows up (Authority/GM/Director/Program Manager/Board "may," plus unreviewable rescission and undefined-standard money/penalty calls). The few truly hard-and-fast, zero-exception rules cluster around **money** (non-refundable fees, check-cashing deadlines, W-9 payment blocks) and **absolute conduct bans** (no volunteer judges, non-motorized vehicles, abusive conduct toward officials) — everything else (penalties, sale-proceeds handling, vehicle use, rescission) is explicitly discretionary with few or no stated criteria.
