## Age/Eligibility
- Minimum age **8**; must not reach 19th birthday by Dec 31 of enrollment year.
- Divisions (as of Dec 31 of current 4-H year): **Junior 8–10, Intermediate 11–13, Senior 14–18**.
- Must be a "bona fide member of Colorado 4-H whose project work and records are certified and up-to-date."
- Must be enrolled in the specific project/unit/level exhibited; members completing two units in one year demonstrating mastery are expected to move to the higher unit (project-specific exceptions may apply).
- Only one entry per class per member; only one county entry accepted per class overall.

## Entry Deadlines & Key Dates (2026 State Fair cycle)
- Entries due **5:00 PM Thursday, August 13** via FairEntry.
- County-assigned delivery time to bring projects to State Fair: **Tuesday, August 18**.
- Judging: **8:00 AM Thursday–Friday, August 20–21**.
- Exhibit takedown: **Tuesday, September 8**, mandatory 9:00 AM arrival, facility closes 1:00 PM.

## Judging System / Scoring Weight
- Standard projects: **25% e-Record quality + 75% exhibit quality**.
- Leadership projects: 50/50 split.
- "American System" used — ranks against product standards/requirement fulfillment, not pure head-to-head comparison.
- Awards: Champion/Reserve Champion, 3rd–10th place, "State Fair Qualifier" (substandard entries), Grand Champion/Reserve Grand Champion (chosen from class champions by age division).

## Documentation/Labeling Rules
- Original-work requirement: work not created by the member is treated as plagiarism.
- AI-generated content must be disclosed with a specific citation format (e.g., "Image generated by DALL-E, Bing, [date], [URL]").
- FairEntry label (Avery 5161) required showing exhibitor name, county, department, division, class, age.
- Printed FairEntry entry card must be attached to exhibit.
- No member home address may be displayed on exhibits (security).

## Display Specs
- Display board: **4 ft wide × 3 ft tall**, lightweight cardboard (poster board not allowed); text readable from 3–5 ft.
- No sharp items (blades, thumbtacks, fishing hooks, knives, etc.).

## Project-Specific Notes Found
- **Cake Decorating:** top-10/grand-champion finishers must move to a new unit next cycle.
- **Photography (Unit 7):** members using advanced photo-editing tools must enter Digital Image Editing class; requires 20 demo pairs (unedited+edited) = 40 photos total.
- **Filmmaking:** video must be under 10 minutes, "G/PG"-appropriate content.

## Liability
- Fair management not responsible for exhibit loss/damage though "all care" will be used.
- Champion/Reserve Champion/placing exhibits must remain on display for the fair's full duration.

*Note: this document is the general "Exhibit Requirements" master covering static/non-livestock exhibit classes broadly (Consumer Science, Family & Consumer Sciences, General/STEM projects, etc.), not a Consumer-Science-only document — consistent with its original filename ("StateFairExhibitReq" = all exhibits, not species/department-specific).*
