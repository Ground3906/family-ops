# Colorado State Fair (CSF) 2026 Exhibitor Handbook — Extract
Source: https://coloradostatefair.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/2026-CSF-Exhibitor-Handbook-Full-TOC.pdf
Archived raw PDF: fair/rulebooks/archive/csf-2026-exhibitor-handbook.pdf (5.0MB, 689 pages, confirmed via pdfinfo/pdftotext)
Fair dates: **August 28 – September 7, 2026**. This document is the COMPLETE handbook (full rule text for every department, not a TOC/index) despite the "-Full-TOC" filename — the filename appears to just mean "Full [book] + TOC" combined in one PDF.

## Cross-Cutting Rules (apply to every department below)
- Authority holds "final and absolute right to interpret" all requirements; Management (GM/Directors/Superintendents) has "discretionary power to interpret rules, enforce safety and grooming standards ... and adjudicate disputes."
- Entry = binding contract + full indemnification of Authority/State of CO/Board/CDA. **Entry fees are non-refundable** (stated verbatim in General, Livestock, and Horse sections).
- W-9 required for any premium/sale ≥$600; no correct W-9 = no payout. Returned checks: $35 fee, 5 business days to cure, exhibitor barred from showing until cured.
- **Discipline** (Determination of Violations §2): Program Manager/Director/GM "may" disqualify, claw back fees/premiums/awards fair-wide (not just the violating division), and bar an exhibitor "for a determined period, including a lifetime suspension."
- **Appeals**: 15-day written notice → 15-day exhibitor appeal window → optional 15-day rescission → hearing panel of 4 (1 Board member presiding, 1 subject-matter expert, 1 public member, 1 senior staffer) → 15-day findings. Third-party **grievances require a $300 bond**.
- Language pattern: numeric/procedural rules use "shall/must" and read as hard cutoffs (deadlines, weights, fees); enforcement and interpretation consistently use "may"/"at its discretion"/"reserves the right" with no exhibitor-side exception path stated.

## Market Livestock

### Cross-species rules (Livestock & Small Animal / Junior Livestock Competition Requirements)
- Premises ID (PID) mandatory at entry for Open/Market livestock (8 CCR 1208-1 Art. 8.4(a)(i)).
- **Market entries close Monday, July 27, 2026** (online only, no mail-in). Open/Jr Breeding entries close Monday, August 10, 2026.
- 4-H/FFA eligibility: age 8 and not yet 19 as of Dec 31 of prior year; full-time CO resident, ≤2 consecutive weeks outside CO between nomination and Fair's end.
- Entry cap: **4 head per species per exhibitor** (stated identically for beef, goats, hogs, lambs, chicken pens, turkeys).
- CVI (health cert) required, dated ≤7 days before arrival, all species.
- "Unethically Fitted Livestock" = 13 enumerated banned practices (drug injection for deception, hide cutting, artificial tail-heads, ice packs, paint except on cattle, etc.) — closed list, "may result in disciplinary action."
- **Re-weigh rule** (identical wording for beef/goats/hogs/lambs): top 5 finishers in each class re-weighed; **>5% deviation from declared weight = disqualified**, no reweigh, not eligible for any other class.
- Drug residue: zero-tolerance carcass standard; ractopamine is the only FDA-approved beta-agonist for cattle/swine/turkeys (0-day withdrawal); non-label use of otherwise-legal drugs is itself a violation.
- **Junior Livestock Sale**: terminal/direct-to-slaughter, **7% commission on gross**. Grand Champion, Reserve Grand Champion, and Division Champions **must** sell (no withdrawal option). Others may withdraw via form within **30 minutes** of qualifier posting. Failure to show a qualified sale animal = forfeiture of **all** awards/premiums/buybacks earned at the Fair **and a 3-year exhibition ban**.
- Sale quotas: up to 40 Beef / 40 Goats / 40 Hogs / 40 Lambs / 2 Chicken pens / 2 Rabbit pens / 2 Turkeys.

### Market Beef (Dept 915) — $25/entry
- Min weight **1,050 lbs** (steers and heifers). Heifers require vet certification of "OPEN" (non-pregnant) within 30 days of the Fair. Entries close Jul 27; arrival Aug 29; show Aug 31.
- Premiums 1st–10th: $40/35/30/25/20/15/10/10/5/5.

### Market Hogs / Swine (Dept 955) — $20/entry
- Weight window **235–300 lbs**. Pseudorabies test required unless CO-origin + current health cert. Breed needs ≥10 head to get its own class (else folded into Other Purebred); breeds with ≤15 head → only Champion/Reserve Champion qualify for Sale. Cordless-clipper touch-up only pre-show; **no oils, paints, powders, or glitter** — hogs "will be wiped for colors."

### Market Lambs / Sheep (Dept 945) — $20/entry
- Weight window **110–165 lbs**. Scrapie Flock ID tag mandatory for all sexually intact sheep ≥18 months. Breed-class minimum 5 head, cascading combination rule if under threshold (Dorset→Speckled Face→Southdown→Black Face). **No evidence of surgical docking/prolapse/purse-strings** permitted — instant disqualification.

### Market Goats (Dept 935) — $20/entry
- Weight window **55–115 lbs** (does or wethers). Must be slick-shorn; horns "tipped blunt, no sharp points allowed." "No family members will be permitted to hold or stage any **lamb**-holding pens at any time. No exceptions" — note: this exact sentence appears verbatim in the Goats section referencing "lamb"-holding pens, an apparent copy/paste artifact from the Lambs section, suggestive of boilerplate reuse across CSF's own species documents.

## Poultry & Turkey (full depth per instructions)

### Market Chickens (Dept 804.3001) — CSF's *only* poultry competition — $10/pen of 3 (same breed)
- Fryer pen: 3.5–7 lbs/bird live weight, **≤8 weeks old**. Broiler pen: 7–12 lbs/bird, **≤20 weeks old**. Must be nominated through CSU Extension office — unnominated birds are "rejected, and no refund will be issued." Terminal show; CSF offers a buyback consignment. CSU Avian Health Team inspects on arrival; illness = excluded from grounds entirely. Exhibitor (not parent/adult) must personally carry birds to the judging table, on pain of disqualification. Entries-tiered premium scale (not flat): 1 entry pays 1st only ($5); 15+ entries pays 1st–10th ($14/12/11/10/9/8/7/6/5/4).

### Market Turkeys (Dept 806.001) — standalone division — $25/bird
- Hen: **13–30 lbs, 16–20 weeks old**. Tom: **20–30 lbs, 16–20 weeks old**. "Turkeys over 30 pounds are ineligible to show." Same CSU-Extension nomination requirement, terminal/buyback structure, and no-parent-carry rule as chickens. Grand/Reserve Champion qualify for Junior Livestock Sale.

### No CSF analog: open/exhibition (breeding, bantam, standard-breed) poultry
CSF runs **no** open, breeding, bantam, or standard-breed poultry show and no egg division — only two junior **terminal market** classes (meat-pen chickens, individual market turkeys) feeding directly into the slaughter sale. If Custer's dormant poultry program is (or becomes) an exhibition/breed-show model rather than a 4-H market-meat project, **there is no CSF state-level analog for that model** — this is a genuine gap, not an oversight.

## Horse Show
- Governed by General Competition Requirements + Horse Show-specific requirements + Colorado 4-H Horse Rulebook (Rev. 2024) + Colorado Miniature Horse Rulebook (Rev. 2026).
- **4-H State Championship Horse Show**: entries close **Aug 1, 2026, 11:59pm**; late window Aug 2–8 (fee discrepancy in source: "$50 per exhibitor" in the narrative text vs. "$75.00" per late entry in the fee table — both appear verbatim in the same document). All other CSF horse shows: pre-entry closes Aug 15; late Aug 16–25 at $50/horse (waived if only adding classes to an already-entered horse).
- Fees: $10/class, $10/written test, $10/office fee. Stalls: early-arrival $60 (whole show) or fair-time $45 (whole show), tack stall $20, single-day stall $25/day.
- Eligibility: 4-H exhibitor age 8, not yet 19 as of Dec 31 of prior year. Division (Novice/Limited/Advanced) is locked by passed competency test *before* entry; "Exhibitors may not switch divisions after the ... registration deadline." **No stallions** permitted. Max 2 horses/exhibitor (4 for miniatures, but only 2 stalls).
- Health: CVI ≤7 days old for all horses. CO-origin horses need no Coggins/EIA test; out-of-state horses need a negative EIA (AGID or ELISA) dated ≤1 year.
- Discretion language: "Management may, at its discretion, bar an entry or person ... if not properly presented"; "There will be no refunds for missed classes" (hard, no stated exception).

## Open Division / Creative Arts (Static Exhibits)
General cross-department rules (2026 General Entry & Fine Arts Dept Requirements): entry window **June 15 – July 22, 2026, 4:00pm hard cutoff**, no late entries; judged on Originality / Suitability of Materials / Creativity / Craftsmanship / Overall Effect; one complimentary day pass per paid exhibitor; checks not accepted after Jul 22.

### Baked goods, food preservation, decorated baked goods → **Pantry Dept** ($2/item; Junior ≤13 / Youth 14-18 / Open 19+)
- Hard rule: entries must be **"made from scratch"** — no mixes; **professionals are barred** from baking classes ("a professional baker is not permitted to enter any baking divisions").
- Canning: home-canned **after July 29, 2025** cutoff (note: this date is >1 year before the Aug 2026 show — likely intentional shelf-life window, possibly a stray typo); standard sealed jars only; **"No steam pressure canners"** for vegetables; **"No paraffin"** on jellies; label ≤3"x3" (class/product/date/altitude/process method — no exhibitor name).
- Cakes: disqualified if entered in the baking pan; **no cake mixes**; **"No cream cheese frostings allowed"**; whole cakes only.
- Cookies: standard 2–3" diameter, but **"Decorated Cookies may be larger than 3 inches"** — this is the closest thing CSF has to a "decorated baked goods" class; there is no standalone cake-decorating/skill division (cakes are judged on taste/crumb, not decoration).
- Kitchen Royalty (adult, CO residents only): compete in ≥12 of 19 named divisions, place in ≥10; 5-year past-winner lockout; awards $200 + rosette + engraved silver bowl. Youth Kitchen Royalty: ≥9 of 19 divisions, place in ≥7; $50 + plaque.

### Floriculture Dept ($5/potted plant, $5/arrangement, $5/herb, $2/cut flower; Junior ≤13/Youth 14-18/Open 19+)
- Must be grown by exhibitor or in exhibitor's possession ≥3 months. Judged on plant condition "not the size," expressly "at the Judge's discretion."

### Horticulture → **Farm and Garden Dept** ($5/item; Open 19+ / Youth 8-18)
- Must be "grown by the exhibitor on the exhibitor's farm, school farm, collective farm, or ... garden during the most recent growing season." Misrepresented produce = disqualified, fees forfeited. Precise per-class display quantities (e.g., beans: 5 pods; tomatoes: 3–5; carrots: 3 with tops).

### Heritage/fiber arts (knitting, crochet, quilting, embroidery) → **Needle Arts** + **Quilts Depts** ($5/item each)
- Needle Arts (Junior≤13/Youth14-18/Open19+/Senior65+): **"All Needle Arts must be sewn. Glue will be accepted only where a sewing technique cannot be used."** Hand Embroidery division explicitly bars **"No counted cross-stitch. No machine embroidery."** Afghans have a hard minimum size: **45"x45", or 45"x60" with fringe.**
- Quilts (separate dept, same 4 age groups): defined as 3 layers (top/batting/backing) and **"must be quilted through all of the ... three layers"**; completed within the last 3 years; previously-CSF-entered quilts barred from re-entry. Duet (2-person) and One-Person are separate class ranges (0311–0344 vs. 0411–0443).

### Scrapbooking → **Arts and Crafts Dept**, Miscellaneous Crafts Division 0081, Class 0291 ("two-page layout, any size")
- Same $5/entry fee and Junior≤13/Youth14-18/Open19+/Senior65+/PWD age-group structure as the rest of Arts & Crafts.

### Sewing/clothing → **Needle Arts Dept**, Clothing Construction Division 0041 & Sewing Other Than Clothing Division 0042
- Recycled-fabric garment class requires "at least 50% recycled fabric." Decorated (purchased) clothing is allowed only if "the decoration must be a sewn technique."

### Jewelry → **Arts and Crafts Dept**, Jewelry Division 0078 (7 classes: non-traditional/fabricated/cast/lampwork/strung/crocheted/original) — **"Must be mounted for display. No kits."** Also present as a single catch-all class (0006) inside the youth "Amateur Art" division.

### Visual arts / Photography → split across two tracks, youth vs. adult
- **Amateur Art** (youth, $5/item; age brackets ≤5 / 6-10 / 11-14 / 15-18): Painting, Drawing, Sculpture, Ceramics, Photography, Jewelry, and a catch-all "Any Other Medium Not Otherwise Listed."
- **Fine Arts Exhibition** (open/juried, all CO residents): $11/piece Emerging Artist, $15/piece Professional; max **5 entries/exhibitor**; juried and **"the jurors' decisions are final"**; work must be original, completed within 2 years, and never before shown at CSF; AI-generated art must be disclosed; **25% commission** on any sale. Photography defined narrowly: **"significant digital modification is not allowed... acceptable modifications include ... cropping, resizing, and slight (20% or less) color correction."**

### Miscellaneous/Other → **Arts and Crafts Dept**, Miscellaneous Crafts Division 0081 (Class 0294, "Any item not otherwise listed"), plus a near-universal "Any item not otherwise listed" catch-all class appended to nearly every division in every creative-arts department (Ceramics, Ag Mechanics, Wood, Leather, etc.).

## Language-Pattern Summary
- **Hard/no-exception numeric rules**: weight windows, the 5%-reweigh tolerance, $600 W-9 threshold, 4-head species cap, 3-year quilt age limit, 45"x45" afghan minimum, canning date cutoff, no-paraffin/no-steam-canner bans, entry-fee non-refundability, "No exceptions" phrasing (goat/lamb holding pens).
- **Discretion clauses** (final say retained by Authority/Management/Judge, no stated exhibitor recourse beyond the general appeal process): "at its/his/her discretion," "reserves the right," "may impose any other appropriate penalty," "at the Judge's discretion," "Colorado State Fair Management will decide on the proper artwork condition."
- Net pattern: exhibitors face rigid, specific numeric compliance thresholds ("shall/must"), while the Authority's enforcement, interpretation, and dispute resolution remains fully discretionary ("may") — CSF's own text sometimes internally conflicts on secondary figures (e.g., horse show late fee stated as both $50 and $75), suggesting these documents are compiled from multiple boilerplate sources per division rather than centrally unified.
