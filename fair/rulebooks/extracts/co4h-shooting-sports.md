## Registration & Deadlines
- State Championship registrations due: **5:00 PM August 1** of the current 4-H year.
- Registration fee: **$20.00/participant**; non-shotgun class fee **$5.00/class**; shotgun class fee **$15.00/class**.
- Edits/cancellations accepted until **5:00 PM August 6**; no refunds for cancellations after that.

## Eligibility & Certification
- **.22 Pistol:** member must be at least **9 years old** as of start of current 4-H year; must have completed ≥1 project year with live-fire experience; requires written parent/guardian permission (including firearm-possession-eligibility attestation).
- **Air Pistol:** contest open only to counties with certified 4-H pistol leaders.

## Division/Class Structure
- **Junior vs. Senior** divisions, numbered by discipline series: 100s = .22 Rifle, 200s = Air Rifle, 208–209 = Air Pistol, 300–304 = Shotgun, 400–408 = Archery, 500 = Muzzleloading (odd numbers = Senior parallel classes).
- **.22 Rifle:** 50' Hunt (open sights), 50' Hunt Scope (max 4x, locked), 50' 4-Position Sporter (peep aperture), 50' International 3-Position (peep aperture, mandatory padded shooting coat).
- **Air Rifle:** 25' Utility (open sights), 10M 4-Position Sporter (peep), 10M Precision 3-Position (peep), 10M Olympic Off-Hand (peep, gun max 12.12 lbs).
- **Air Pistol:** 10-meter; Juniors shoot bench + two-hand standing; Seniors shoot two-hand + one-hand standing.
- **.22 Pistol:** Camp Perry Round Modified (25 yd, 40 record shots: 10 slow/15 timed/15 rapid); Hunter Silhouette (40–100 yd, 20 record shots).
- **Shotgun:** Sporting Clays (50 targets); American Skeet (2×25); Trap (2×25). Max gauge 12; max shell length 2-3/4"; max shot load 1-1/8 oz.
- **Archery:** Compound Unlimited/Limited/Traditional; Recurve Limited/Traditional; Modified FITA + 3-D combined scoring. Max draw weight 60 lbs.
- **Muzzleloading:** single class, both divisions combined.
- **Team size (Shotgun & Archery):** max 5 competitors/team; top 4 individual scores count toward county/combined-county team score.

## Health/Safety Equivalent (Range Safety) Requirements
- Clear Bore Indicators must extend ≥3" past muzzle and action when not firing.
- Mandatory eye protection for all competitors/personnel near ranges; mandatory hearing protection.
- No reloaded ammunition. .22 rifle: no .22 magnum, no hollow-point/hyper-velocity (1,400+ fps)/incendiary/tracer rounds.
- AR/Modern Sporting-type firearms **not allowed**; suppressors/muzzle brakes **not permitted**; no set or electric triggers (rifle).
- Air Rifle: .177 cal lead wadcutter pellets only.
- .22 Rifle Hunt class: max 7.5 lbs, .675" max barrel diameter, factory stock. 4-P Sporter max 8.5 lbs. Int'l 3-P: no weight limit.

## Course of Fire
- **.22 Rifle:** 10 shots per position (Prone, Standing, Kneeling, Sitting); sight-in 5 min, record period 40 min.
- **Air Rifle:** 5 shots per position; sight-in 5 min, record period 30 min.
- **Archery Modified FITA:** Juniors — 40/30/20/10 yd, 3 arrows/distance (12 total/120 pts); Seniors — 50/40/30/20 yd, 5 arrows/distance (20 total/200 pts); max 5 min/distance.
- **Archery 3-D:** Juniors — 2 arrows × 4 targets (8/80 pts); Seniors — 2 arrows × 10 targets (20/200 pts); max 2 min/target.

## Judging/Scoring System
- **Orion Scoring System** used for rifle, air rifle, pistol, shotgun, archery; decimal scoring for air rifle; shots touching higher ring score up.
- Discipline-specific tie-breaker hierarchies specified (e.g., .22 Rifle: Standing → Kneeling → Prone → Sitting → most 10s).

## Coaching Rule
- Coaching allowed only during sight-in period; coach may call shots/check time/score/order sight changes but may **not** physically assist with loading, cocking, position adjustment, or sight correction. Max 1 coach on line during sight-in. No coaching from spectator area (exception: cocking assistance with prior superintendent approval).

## Dress Code
- International 3-P: mandatory shooting coat/jacket + gloves; pads ≤3/4" thick; sleeves no longer than wrist.
- Most other classes: shooting coats/jackets/gloves/pants/shoes are **prohibited**.
