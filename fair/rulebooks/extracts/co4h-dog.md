# Colorado 4-H State Fair Dog Trials/Show Requirements — Extract

**County book cites:** https://co4h.colostate.edu/statefair/StateFairContestReq-Dog.pdf (now DEAD — redirects to generic `https://co4h.colostate.edu/4-h-resources/` landing page, HTTP 200 on landing page)
**Current working source:** https://co4h.colostate.edu/wp-content/uploads/sites/27/2026/05/State-4-H-Dog-Trials-2026.pdf (titled "2026 State 4-H Dog Trials"; page hub: https://co4h.colostate.edu/4h-resource/4-h-state-fair-dog-trial-requirements/)
**Archived copy:** fair/rulebooks/archive/co4h-dog.pdf
**Scope note:** This single document covers **Showmanship (conformation), Obedience, Rally, and Agility** — i.e., it is the modern successor to the old "Contest Req - Dog" doc and includes the Dog Show/showmanship component the county book references, not just performance trials.

## Key Deadlines
- Entry deadline: **5:00 PM Monday, August 10, 2026**.
- Dog ID must be complete in 4-H Online by **May 1** or dog cannot compete.
- Entry fees mailed by **September 30** to CSU Extension.

## Event Dates/Venues
- Weekend 1 (**Aug 29–30**): Showmanship, Obedience, Rally — Douglas County Fairgrounds, Castle Rock.
- Weekend 2 (**Sept 26–27**): Agility — El Paso County Fairgrounds, Calhan.

## Health/Vaccination Requirements
- Current rabies vaccination certificate mandatory; **titers not accepted, no exceptions**.
- Distemper & parvovirus vaccination within 3 years, OR serum titers.
- Bordetella "strongly encouraged but not required."
- Dogs with physical evidence of respiratory disease refused admission.
- Age minimums: Foundation agility 12 months; all other agility 15 months; all other classes 6 months.

## Exhibitor Eligibility
- Age divisions (as of Dec 31): Junior 8–10, Intermediate 11–13, Senior 14–18.
- Must be enrolled in 4-H Dog Project; must own dog individually/with family/guardian.
- Primary care requirement: member must provide care/decision-making ≥51% of project duration.
- **Leasing a dog is not permitted.**

## Qualifying Scores
- Showmanship ≥170/200; Obedience ≥170; Rally ≥70; Agility ≥70.

## Class Structure & Entry Caps
- **Showmanship:** Novice/Open/Advanced by age group; max 4 entries/county/class (Master Showman unlimited, requires prior Grand/Reserve Grand Champion).
- **Obedience:** Pre-Beginner Novice/Beginner Novice/Novice capped at 4 entries; Graduate Novice/Advanced tiers unlimited; A/B level system, must advance after qualifying score.
- **Rally:** Novice–Advanced/Excellent capped at 4; Masters unlimited; Team Rally = 2 team entries/county (4 exhibitors/team).
- **Agility:** unlimited entries/class; Foundation/Novice/Intermediate/Advanced × Regular/Veteran; Jumpers & Standard course types.
- Mandatory advancement rule: qualifying score in Obedience/Rally requires moving up the following year (exceptions: top "B"-level classes).
- External-title rule: any outside national-association Rally title disqualifies the dog from that class or lower (unless earned <4 months before State Trials).

## Judging/Scoring
- Showmanship: written test (zeroed if missed) + ring performance.
- Obedience/Rally: 15-pt deduction for harsh correction/excessive barking/leaving ring; 15-pt fouling deduction.
- Agility: perfect = 100 ("Clean Run"); Standard Course Time-based tiebreak; elimination for poor sportsmanship/abuse/aggression/fouling/exceeding max course time.
- Ties broken by lowest recorded time.

## Conduct/Prohibitions
- Business-casual recommended; no flip-flops/sandals/open-toed shoes in ring (5-min grace period to change).
- Hybrid (wolf/coyote) dogs prohibited; no dog tags during ring performance; no halties/gentle leaders/harnesses/pinch collars in Obedience/Rally; no dogs in heat/pregnant/lactating; no selling dogs on fairgrounds.
- Aggressive/biting dogs immediately removed. Protest fee **$50** (refunded if upheld).
- Volunteer need noted: ≥18 volunteers Saturday, ≥15 Sunday.
