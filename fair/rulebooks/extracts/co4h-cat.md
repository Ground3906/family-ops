## Cat Show Section — Full Detail
- **Superintendent:** Marlena Griesse.
- **Date:** Saturday, **September 5, 2026**. Check-in 11:00 AM–12:30 PM; show starts 1:00 PM.
- **Entry fee:** $20/4-H member.
- **Registration deadline: August 13.**

### Health/Vaccination Requirements
- Rabies vaccination required, current, administered by a licensed veterinarian only.
- FVRCP (feline viral rhinotracheitis, calicivirus, panleukopenia) vaccination required.
- Feline Leukemia / FIV testing-vaccination "highly recommended" (not mandatory).
- Proof of vaccination required on the **State 4-H Cat ID Form by May 1**.

### Cat Eligibility
- Purebred or household pet cats both eligible.
- No pregnant/lactating females.
- Minimum age **4 months**.
- Must be clean/healthy, clear eyes/nose, free of injury/parasites/fleas/ticks/ear mites; front and rear claws clipped.
- Cats vet-checked at check-in; unhealthy animals dismissed on the spot.

### Exhibitor Eligibility
- Enrolled in 4-H Cat Project; must own cat individually or with immediate family (no leasing); must provide majority of care; must be official county representative; acceptable record book required.

### Age Divisions (as of Dec 31 prior year)
- Junior 8–10, Intermediate 11–13, Senior 14–18.

### Class Structure
- **Cage Decorating:** Class 201 Junior / 202 Intermediate / 203 Senior.
- **Conformation** (unlimited cats/youth, one class per cat): Class 204 Purebred / 205 Domestic Short Hair / 206 Domestic Long Hair.
- **Showmanship** (mandatory, one cat/exhibitor): Class 207 Junior / 208 Intermediate / 209 Senior. Judged on handling from cage to judge's table plus knowledge of breed/age/immunization dates/sex/general care.

### Conduct
- Dress code: clean/simple/neat clothing; collared shirt/blouse preferred; pants + closed-toe shoes mandatory.
- Animal mistreatment → dismissal; negative conduct toward officials → disqualification + forfeiture of awards.
- Protest fee **$50** (refunded if upheld).
