# Working Group Change Log

Matt's carry copy. The working group holds the Version 1.0 book built 8/17/2026. This file lists every change locked after that build, keyed to **their** citation numbers so an item raised in the meeting can be found by the number in front of them.

**Scope.** This log is true only against the version the working group currently holds. It retires the prior log, which covered the round they already reviewed and gave feedback on — this round is that feedback, built. When they adopt or reject this round, this file retires in turn and a fresh log opens against whatever version they hold next. Do not carry rows forward.

**How to use it in the meeting.** They raise an issue, find the row by their citation, agree, read the proposed change.

| CC 2.2 | Second paragraph: for market livestock, entry is completed by presenting the animal at the county weigh-in. | Struck. Market entry mechanics move to the new CC 3.1.4a, where the entry limit lives. | Requested directly. The mechanics belong with the limit they now serve, not floating alone in the general entry rule. |

Gang symbols and affiliation get their own standalone sentence next to the other prohibited-message clauses. The baggy-or-tight clause is struck. Every other rule in the paragraph is kept, reordered so bare-skin rules sit together and the tank-top permission sits with the halter and tube-top ban it modifies. | The old sentence only banned baggy-or-tight clothing that also displayed a gang symbol, so a plain gang symbol fell through the gap. | CC 2.11, CC 2.23 | CC 2.11 states duties for one cleanup and mentions a stall-assignment posting that only makes sense pre-fair, while also citing "cleanup dates" plural. CC 2.23 separately requires stall cleanup during the post-fair period. Neither states who runs the post-fair grounds work, and a non-livestock exhibitor reading CC 2.11 cannot tell if the post-fair cleanup reaches them. | CC 2.11 becomes pre-fair cleanup only, restated to cover every exhibitor including non-livestock. CC 2.23 gains the general post-fair grounds-work language and keeps the stall-specific work it already had. | Found while confirming CC 2.11 covers both cleanups. It only ever fully covered one. |
| CC 2.14 | Vet examines the animal on arrival for health. | Examination now explicitly includes body condition. | Closes the gap the fair-wide gain rule (CC 3.1.9a) opened: an animal with no initial weigh-in has nothing that rule can check, so the arrival exam is the one point every animal passes through regardless. |
| CC 3.1 (new, 3.1.4a) | No entry limit exists for market beef, swine, sheep, or goat. An exhibitor may bring more than one animal of the same species. | One market animal per species, per exhibitor. That animal is the exhibitor's entry once weighed in; no substitution, no addition after. One additional non-market animal of the same species is permitted, counted across every non-market class for that species. Scoped to beef, swine, sheep, goat. Poultry and rabbit keep their own limits, now 2 pens/entries each (see below). | Barn space. The single largest structural change this round. |
| CC 3.1 (new, 3.1.5a) | Blanket, cover, and halter are named as prohibited at weigh-in; leg wraps are not named. | Elevated to a shared-layer principle: nothing on the animal at weigh-in beyond required identification. Blanket, cover, halter, and leg wrap print as named examples, stated as examples and not the limit of what's covered. | Leg wraps were the immediate ask, but a named list can only ever ban what someone thought to name. The principle closes the class of problem, not just the one instance. |
| CC 3.1 (new, 3.1.9a, 3.1.9b) | No fair-wide rule ties Grand Drive eligibility to weight. Swine alone bars an out-of-window animal from selling; sheep and goat bar it from showing at all. | Two new shared rules. 9a: fair weight must exceed initial weigh-in weight where an initial weight exists (initial weigh-in stays optional; no weight recorded means nothing to check). 9b: an animal outside its weight window is barred from the Grand Drive only. It still shows, in the appropriate weight class, and still sells under its species rule. Reaches beef, swine, sheep, goat. | Requested: showing is permissive, selling is restrictive, the weight window protects the buyer. Swine already worked this way; sheep and goat did not and it was a drafting inconsistency, not a decision. |
| CC 3.1.10 | Underweight animal released "following the feeder class." | "Following the lightweight class." | Confirmed there is no separate feeder class; it was loose language for the lightweight class all along. Cleanup, not a rule change. |
| CC 3.1.12, CC 6.1.4 | Pen guarantee, seniority order, and capacity valve stated. No-mixing rule stated for market only (CC 3.1.12), absent from non-market (CC 6.1.4). | Absolute no-mixing clause added to both: an assigned pen, stall, or cage holds only that exhibitor's animals, in every case, including under the capacity valve. | Requested for market; extended to non-market for the same reason it exists at all — a kid's assigned space is theirs, full stop. |
| CC 3.2.2a (new) | No weight or sale-eligibility rule exists for market beef. | New rule: minimum weight cited to Addendum §5, no maximum. Under-minimum shows in the lightweight class, does not sell. | Beef had no equivalent to swine's three-tier structure at all. Filled the same gap CSF alignment closed for sheep and goat. |
| CC 3.2.9 (new) | No section exists. | Stub reserved in reading-order position: "[Text pending. Reserved for the Bred and Fed class...]" Not to print in an adopted edition until filled. | Requested. Zero-source posture: log the gap, do not invent eligibility, verification, or award structure that wasn't given. |
| CC 3.3.2a (new) | No ear-notch or identification rule exists for market swine. | New rule adopting the CSF seven-day ear-notch requirement and the Universal Ear Notching System, explicitly declining the registration-paper-match and DNA provisions CSF also carries. Cites the county tag requirement (CC 3.1.7) immediately after. | Requested, framed as education not just enforcement. CSF carries three tiers of this requirement; only the identification tier was adopted. |
| CC 3.3.5 | Allows two of an exhibitor's own animals in one pen; sizes the swine pen guarantee accordingly. | Both sentences struck. Rule now reads only: pen space assigned under CC 3.1.12. | Item 5's one-animal cap makes two-per-pen impossible. Old text preserved above in the renumber table's spirit — full text is CC 3.3.5's committed history in `draft.md`'s git log if it needs to come back before adoption. |
| CC 3.4.4 | Lamb weigh-in preparation rule, blanket/cover/halter only, no leg wraps. | Dissolved. Content is now the shared CC 3.1.5a above. | Superseded by the shared-layer elevation. |
| CC 3.4.8 | Titled "Maximum Weight." States a ceiling with no source, bars an over-ceiling animal from showing entirely, allows it to sell capped. | Retitled "Weight and Sale Eligibility." Minimum and maximum both cited to Addendum §5. Under-minimum shows in lightweight class, does not sell. Over-maximum shows in heaviest class, sells capped. | Matches the swine pattern exactly, and closes the contradiction with the newly-adopted CSF maximum (165 lbs) — the old rule would have started actually barring animals from showing the moment a real number was entered. |
| CC 3.4 (new, 3.4.8a) | No age or dentition rule exists for market sheep. | New rule: milk teeth required at weigh-in, vet dental check available. Word-for-word parallel to the existing goat rule at CC 3.5.8. | Requested, researched: milk teeth are the objective under-one-year marker (lamb vs. mutton), goat already had this exact rule, sheep didn't. Parallel-rules doctrine. |
| CC 3.5.3 | Same "Maximum Weight" shape and same contradiction as old CC 3.4.8. | Same rewrite as CC 3.4.8: "Weight and Sale Eligibility," both bounds cited, under-minimum shows-only, over-maximum shows-and-sells-capped. | Same reasoning as CC 3.4.8, applied to goat. |
| CC 3.5.9 | Goat-specific 5 lb minimum gain requirement. | Struck. Superseded by the shared CC 3.1.9a (fair weight must exceed initial weight), which reaches all four large-animal species rather than goat alone. | The Grossardt case (2026 hog, gained then lost weight over the project period) is the animal-welfare evidence behind the shared rule; goat's old 5 lb figure is folded into the "must exceed" standard rather than kept as a separate number. |
| CC 3.6.3 | States market poultry is terminal and "shall not leave the fairgrounds alive," which is factually false — birds are loaded live and slaughtered off-site. No provision exists for a bird that doesn't sell. | Rewritten in two paragraphs. First states the terminal fact accurately (goes to slaughter regardless of sale) and the custody chain (Authority delivers to the processor's truck; no live release after weigh-in closes). Second adds: a bird that doesn't sell is purchased by the designated floor buyer at floor market price, exhibitor credited. | The false sentence was flagged directly. The buyback commitment was a separate ask that turned out to be the condition on fixing the false sentence honestly — a bird that doesn't sell still needs somewhere to go and someone to pay for it. |
| CC 3.6.4 | No requirement that a market bird actually be weighed. | Adds: every bird must be weighed at fair weigh-in; an unweighed bird is not entered and does not show. | Closes the gap under the new floor-buyer commitment — the buyer pays by the pound, so an unweighed bird has no basis for payment. |
| CC 3.6.12 | "Maximum entries per class" — different unit than rabbit's "per exhibitor," and set at 4. | Rewritten to "per exhibitor, across the department," counted in the units CC 3.6.4 defines (a chicken pen of three, a single turkey). | CC 3.7.2 | Adopts CSF's Grand-and-Reserve-only sale qualification for market rabbit. | Custer does not adopt that limit. | CC 3.7.10 | "Maximum pens per exhibitor," value 4. | Value drops to 2. Unit already matched CC 3.6.12's fix. | Requested. |
| CC 4.1 (new, 4.1.5) | No single rule states everything that governs whether an animal may sell; the facts are scattered across CC 4.1, CC 4.2, and each species chapter. | New index rule listing every governing citation in one place, including the four species weight-and-sale rules above. CC 4.3.1 cites this index rather than restating the list. | Falls out of rewriting the declaration rule — a family declaring an animal needs to know in one place whether it's eligible, and the individual species rules were about to be cited piecemeal in CC 4.3.1's own text. |
| CC 4.3 (whole subchapter) | "Exit Election." An exhibitor who does NOT want to sell must notify by deadline; anything not withdrawn is consigned by default. | Retitled "Consignment and the Sale Declaration." | CC 7.1.6 | Judging criteria stated as a closed list the judge must apply; conformation explicitly excluded. | The four listed criteria (fitting, presentation, showman's appearance, sportsmanship) become what a judge "generally considers," with discretion on how much each counts. Grooming-score tie-breaker paragraph untouched. | Requested: a printed judge shouldn't be boxed into a standard set by a different judge in a different year. |

|---|---|
| New §1 | The IAFE Code, which the book cites by item number throughout but never printed. Custer's paraphrase, attributed to IAFE, with a signature acknowledgment mirroring CSF's. Not a verbatim reprint — copyright policy requires paraphrase even with full attribution. |
| §2 roster split | "Fair and 4-H office" retires. Two real places now: the fair-week Fair office and the year-round CSU Extension office, routed by timing. Phone columns added to Fair Board, Extension/FFA, and Superintendents; blanks are an accepted outcome where someone declines. New tables for 4-H club leaders (one row per leader), designated processors, and floor buyers by species — all unpopulated, all working-group or superintendent input. |
| §5 weights | Every figure lined to the 2026 CSF handbook, verified live against the site (byte-identical, confirmed current). Sheep and goat gain real maximums for the first time (165, 115 lbs). Poultry ages added. Entry limits drop to 2 for both poultry and rabbit, unit-matched. |

Notice-only, no signature field. §7.1's dress-code text inside the Code of Conduct rewritten to match CC 2.4 word for word, so the two don't drift. §7.3 (animal care and housing form) now routes to the CSU Extension office, correcting a standing inconsistency where this one form disagreed with every other citation to it. |
| Two pre-existing defects caught and fixed | §4's deadline table said "Times and locations... are in §7," which pointed at Grievance and Appeal even before tonight's renumber — Schedule was always §2 (now §3). Separately, the Code of Conduct's grievance paragraph cited Addendum §6 for fees when Fees was actually §5 at the time — both now point at the correct section under the new numbers. Neither was caused by this round; both surfaced while touching the same text for the renumber. |
