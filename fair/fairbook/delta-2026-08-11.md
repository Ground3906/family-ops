## CC1-AUTHORITY — CC 1.4, the Authority (BOOK TEXT, confirmed 8/11/2026)

Finished text, from FB-AUTHORITY-02 (delta 8/10). Confirmed by Matt as book text this session.

> **CC 1.4 The Authority**
>
> Custer County acknowledges and adopts the Colorado State Fair General Competition Requirements' use of "the Authority" as the body that administers the fair and enforces its rules. The Colorado State Fair is a single statutory body. Custer County is not, so the term is defined here by role.
>
> **CC 1.4.1** Each person or body listed below is the Authority when acting within their own role. No person acts as the Authority outside it.
>
> (a) The Fair Board, in all matters, and as the final interpreter of these rules.
>
> (b) A superintendent, in their own department, as lead for classes, weights, show order, and running the show.
>
> (c) A judge, in the class being judged, while judging it.
>
> (d) The licensed veterinarian, in animal health, fitness, and slaughter readiness. The veterinarian may refuse or excuse any animal.
>
> (e) The CSU Extension agent and the FFA advisor, in membership, eligibility, enrollment records, and conduct.
>
> **CC 1.4.2** Where these rules say the Authority decides, the decision belongs to whichever person in CC 1.4.1 is competent to make it. The veterinarian does not rule on showmanship. The judge does not rule on a health certificate.

- **Status:** DISCUSS-AT-WALKTHROUGH (session-authored definition, no county analog).
- **Section number provisional** pending the full CC 1 definitions pass.

**Source correction, 8/11/2026.** The 8/10 entry states the membership gate prints "ONCE, market livestock only." **This is wrong.** It prints four times with three different scopes:
