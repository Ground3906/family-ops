# Board Briefs

Board-facing writeups for findings that produce **no rule change**, or where the board should understand why the book reads the way it does before it votes.

**Lane.** This file is board-facing. Collaborative register, evidence-backed, no named individuals, no attribution of a past problem to any person. It is not the working-group change log at `wg-change-log.md`, which tracks proposed rule changes against the version the working group holds and retires when they vote. Entries here survive that retirement and carry forward to the adoption vote.

**How to use it.** Each entry states the question raised, what the research found, and what was decided. Read the entry, not a summary of it, when the topic comes up.

---

## Danish and American judging systems

**Raised.** Whether the Fair Book should move every competition to the American system and eliminate the Danish system, on the expectation that a single system would simplify the book.

**What the research found.**

The book is already almost entirely American. CC 2.21 establishes the American System as the default for every competitive class, with Danish stated as the exception. The Danish system survives in exactly two places:

- **CC 6.4, Dog.** Merit ribbons in obedience and showmanship classes. The point ranges are set on score sheets published by the Colorado State 4-H Office and are cited to the Colorado State 4-H Dog Rules, Obedience General Rules, Rule 12.
- **CC 9, Family and Consumer Sciences and General Projects.** All exhibits in the chapter, under the adopted Colorado State 4-H General Exhibit and Fair Awards standard.

The 2026 county book carried the same split. This is not something the rewrite introduced.

**The Colorado State Fair is not a guide here.** A full-text search of the 2026 CSF Exhibitor Handbook returns no competitive use of the Danish system anywhere. The only two occurrences in 689 pages are a cabbage variety and a pastry variety. So this is not a CSF alignment question in either direction.

**What conversion would cost.** Both remaining uses rest on Colorado State 4-H documents the county does not control. Converting either would put a county rule in conflict with the state 4-H standard the project is judged under, and a member taking a general project or a dog project to a state event would be judged one way at the county fair and another way at state.

**The systems are not interchangeable.** Danish rates each exhibit against the standard set for its project. American ranks exhibits against one another. In a class of twelve indoor exhibits, American produces one blue, one red, one white, and nine members with no ribbon, regardless of the quality of the work.

**Decided.** No change. Danish stays in CC 6.4 and CC 9. American governs everywhere else, as it already does. This is recorded as a deliberate decision so it is not rediscovered as an oversight.

**Open for board discussion.** Whether the county wishes to depart from the Colorado State 4-H standard in these two departments is a policy question the board may take up. Nothing in the book prevents it. The cost of doing so is stated above.
