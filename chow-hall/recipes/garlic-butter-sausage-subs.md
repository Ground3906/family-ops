# Garlic Butter Sausage Subs

**Source:** Delish
**Linked meal:** Garlic butter sausage subs
**Serves:** 4

### Ingredients

- 1/4 cup (1/2 stick) unsalted butter, room temperature
- 2 cloves garlic, finely grated
- 1/4 cup freshly chopped parsley
- 1/4 tsp kosher salt
- 4 sub rolls
- 1 Tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
- 1 lb ground Italian sausage
- 2 cups marinara sauce
- 8 oz shredded mozzarella cheese
- 1/4 cup thinly sliced basil leaves

### Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 450 degrees. In a small bowl, combine butter with garlic, parsley, and salt. Split rolls lengthwise leaving a hinge on the back and place on a rimmed baking sheet. Spread cut sides of rolls with butter mixture. Bake until golden, about 8 minutes.

2. Meanwhile, heat oil in a large skillet. Add sausage and cook, breaking up large pieces with a spoon and stirring occasionally, until golden brown, about 7 minutes. Add marinara sauce and cook stirring until warmed throughout, about 2 minutes.

3. Spoon sausage mixture into toasted rolls and top with cheese. Return to the oven and cook until cheese is melted, about 7 minutes. Sprinkle with basil and serve.

### Notes

- At altitude (9,000 ft) rolls may toast faster - check at 6 minutes.
- Ground pork from farm stock works as a substitute for Italian sausage - add Italian seasoning, fennel seed, and red pepper flake to season.
- Marinara from pantry or home canned works fine.
