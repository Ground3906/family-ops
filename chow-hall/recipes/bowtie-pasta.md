## Parmesan Garlic Butter Bowtie Pasta

**Source:** Barber Foods / Courtney's Sweets
**Linked meal:** Bowtie noodle, kielbasa, vegetables
**Serves:** Family (scale as needed)

### Ingredients

- 1 box bowtie pasta
- 1 bulb garlic, chopped
- 3 tablespoons butter
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 handful parsley, chopped
- Salt and pepper, to taste
- 3 tablespoons Parmesan cheese (plus more for topping)

### Instructions

1. Boil pasta and drain, reserving about 1 cup of pasta water.
2. In a large skillet, brown garlic in butter and olive oil.
3. Once garlic is softened, toss in the bowties.
4. Sprinkle in parsley, salt, and Parmesan. Add pasta water until all pasta is dressed.

### Notes

- Pairs well with kielbasa sliced and browned in the same skillet before the garlic step.
- Add frozen vegetables to the pasta water in the last 2 minutes of boiling to keep it one pot.
- At altitude (9,000 ft) increase pasta boil time by 2-3 minutes.
