## ALTITUDE DOCTRINE — 9,000 ft (Westcliffe, CO)

**This section is mandatory context for every Chow Hall recipe and cooking instruction.**

At 9,000 feet, atmospheric pressure is significantly lower than sea level. This changes how heat transfers, how liquids behave, and how leavening agents work. Every recipe from a cookbook, website, or box assumes sea level. Chow Hall must apply these adjustments automatically.

---

### Why It Matters

| Condition | Sea Level | 9,000 ft |
|---|---|---|
| Water boils at | 212°F | ~194°F |
| Air pressure | 14.7 psi | ~10.9 psi |
| Evaporation rate | Normal | Significantly faster |
| Leavening expansion | Normal | Faster — can over-rise then collapse |

---

### Oven Baking Adjustments (All Leavened Items — Cakes, Breads, Muffins, Cookies)

| What | Sea Level | 9,000 ft Adjustment |
|---|---|---|
| Oven temperature | Recipe temp | **+25°F** (metal pans); no increase for glass pans |
| Bake time | Recipe time | **Reduce 5–8 min per 30 min of recipe time** |
| Baking powder / soda | Recipe amount | **Reduce by ⅛ tsp per tsp** |
| Sugar | Recipe amount | **Reduce 1–2 tbsp per cup** |
| Liquid | Recipe amount | **Increase 3–4 tbsp per cup** |
| Flour | Recipe amount | **Add 1–2 tbsp per cup** for structure |
| Pan fill level | Up to ⅔ full | **Never over ½ full** — batter rises faster and higher |
| Yeast doughs | Standard rise | **Shorter rise times — punch down earlier**, reduce yeast slightly |

**ProBake Convection on COOK-01 helps significantly at altitude** — rear element + fan creates stable heat envelope that compensates for some altitude expansion issues.

---

### Stovetop / Boiling / Simmering Adjustments

| Method | Adjustment |
|---|---|
| Boiling pasta, grains, beans, eggs | **Cook longer** — water is cooler at a "rolling boil" (194°F not 212°F) |
| Braising / simmering meats | **Add 25% more time** vs. sea-level recipe |
| Candy making / caramel | **Reduce target temperature by ~18°F** (soft ball = ~216°F not 234°F) |
| Deep frying | **Reduce oil temperature by ~18°F** — food browns faster than it cooks through |
| Pressure cooker | **Increase pressure time ~15–20%** — boiling point inside is still lower |

---

### Dry Heat / Oven Roasting

Oven roasting meats and vegetables at high altitude behaves nearly the same as sea level. **No adjustment needed for roasting temperatures.** Monitor for increased surface drying/browning — tent with foil if needed. Internal temp probe (COOK-01 has one) removes the guesswork entirely.

---

### Altitude Rule of Thumb for Chow Hall
1. **Baking anything leavened?** Apply all five adjustments above before suggesting a recipe.
2. **Roasting meat?** No temp adjustment. Use internal probe. Standard times.
3. **Boiling / simmering?** Add time. Water never gets hot enough fast enough.
4. **Recipe from a box or commercial source?** Check label for high altitude instructions. Many have them printed.
