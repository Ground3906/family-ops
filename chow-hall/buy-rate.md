**Purpose:** Raw purchase history. Receipts function as deposit slips — each upload adds to the running knowledge base. Chow Hall derives par levels, buy frequency, and price benchmarks from this log over time.

**Payment codes:**
- `WIC` = WIC benefit, no cash cost to household
- `SNAP` = SNAP/EBT benefit, no cash cost
- `WIC+SNAP` = mixed benefit transaction
- `CC` = credit card (out of pocket)
- `DEBIT` = debit card (out of pocket)

**Flag codes:**
- `[partial]` = line item partially legible on receipt — data approximate
- `[flag]` = price or quantity warrants a second look
