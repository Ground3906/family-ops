# Livestock Housing Plans — 3 Pigs + 3 Lambs, Summer 2027

Working doctrine for housing 3 market pigs and 3 market lambs, comparing a 40' cargo container vs. the garage. Both options below are scaled, dimension-checked drafts committed as starting points — not yet built.

---

## Container Option 1

**Container interior (confirmed):** 39'6" L x 7'8" W — NOT the rounded 40'x8' exterior figure.

**Layout:** Pig band runs full length along one long wall (4' deep), split into 4 equal pens. Side walkway (3'8" wide) runs the full length alongside, starting right at the door — no separate staging zone. Merged Pen 4/5 sits as its own full-width block at the back.

| Element | Dimensions | Area |
|---|---|---|
| Pig 1 | 4' x 8'4.5" | ~33.4 sq ft |
| Pig 2 | 4' x 8'4.5" | ~33.4 sq ft |
| Pig 3 | 4' x 8'4.5" | ~33.4 sq ft |
| Pig 4 | 4' x 8'4.5" | ~33.4 sq ft |
| Merged Pen 4/5 | 7'8" x 6' | 46 sq ft |
| Side walkway | 3'8" x 33'6" | — |

**Length check:** (4 x 8'4.5") + 6' = 33'6" + 6' = 39'6" ✓
**Width check:** 4' (pens) + 3'8" (walkway) = 7'8" ✓

**Open items on this design:**
- Merged Pen 4/5's use/purpose not yet assigned (Jill/Tim to determine — noted as "leave as is" per session decision)
- No side aisle past the pen row's own footprint — full-width pens accessed off the walkway in sequence
- Ventilation and floor treatment still unaddressed — steel box, no airflow plan yet

## Garage Option 1

**Garage:** 23' x 23' (529 sq ft)

**Layout:** Pig pens on left wall (3 pens), pig pen + sheep pen on right wall, both flush to the top wall. Center aisle divides the two sides. Open floor consolidated at the bottom.

| Element | Dimensions | Area |
|---|---|---|
| Pig 1 (left wall) | 5' x 10' | 50 sq ft |
| Pig 2 (left wall) | 5' x 10' | 50 sq ft |
| Pig 3 (left wall) | 5' x 10' | 50 sq ft |
| Pig 4 (right wall) | 5' x 10' | 50 sq ft |
| Sheep pen (right wall) | 10' x 10' | 100 sq ft |
| Center aisle | 3' wide x 23' | — |
| Open floor (bottom) | 23' x 8' | 184 sq ft |

**Width check:** 10' (left) + 3' (aisle) + 10' (right) = 23' ✓
**Depth check:** 15' (pens) + 8' (open floor) = 23' ✓ (both walls)
