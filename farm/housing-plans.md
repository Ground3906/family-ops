# Livestock Housing: Garage Barn (LOCKED 2026-08-02)

**Mission:** 3 market hogs + 3 market lambs, summer 2027 fair year. Occupancy Feb-Jul. Temporary solution, ~3 year horizon, until the purpose-built barn after graduation and job landing.

## The Building
- 23x23 slab (529 sq ft), 9' flat ceiling, insulated envelope, insulated overhead door panels
- West wall: house side. Freezer fleet relocates here (measured, fits). Water punch-through from pressure tank directly behind this wall. Electrical panel behind this wall; spare circuits available.
- NW corner: propane supply (heater tees here)
- East wall: 3' concrete stem wall, pen row backer. Two 3x2 sliders (one existing, one new on the south half)
- North wall: 3' concrete stem wall
- South wall: two 10x8 insulated overhead doors (tracks own the south ceiling zone)

## Pens
Five 4x10 panel pens, linked in a row against the east stem wall, gates on the 4' ends. Three hog singles, sheep split 2 and 1. Pen-by-pen species assignment decided at stocking.

## Systems (as built spec)
1. **Exhaust:** Hessaire 16SF3-H 16" gravity-louver shutter fan, through-wall NE corner, blocked into a 24" OC bay. Timer control: long duty summer, dialed down winter. Sized ~900 CFM altitude-corrected requirement; unit delivers ~1,400-1,700 through louvers.
2. **Makeup air:** two east-wall 3x2 sliders + south door perimeter leakage. New window sits south half of east wall so intake sweeps the pen row before the NE fan.
3. **Circulation:** DuraFan 18" sealed-motor fans x5. Three ceiling-hung over north pens, two east-wall mounted over south pens (door tracks preclude ceiling there).
4. **Water:** PEX from pressure tank through west wall, ceiling run to manifold, five drops to Hog Slat AquaChief cup units panel-mounted on gating. Blowout fitting at supply end, low-point fall, line drained outside occupancy. Room heat is freeze defense; insulated envelope + animal heat buy outage hours; compressor blowout is the backstop.
5. **Heat:** Modine HD30 LP unit heater, single-pipe side-wall vent, high-altitude orifice, wall thermostat. Target 60 F. Delivers ~17k BTU/hr at 9,000 ft vs ~20k design load: holds target in normal Feb, sags to low-50s in design-cold snaps, acceptable to stock and water. Animal BTU contribution grows with the season as lows retreat.
6. **Floor:** ArmorClad 100% solids epoxy + primer + 2-pt military urethane topcoat with non-skid, over diamond-ground slab. Covers floor and east/north stem walls (667 sq ft). Seals slab against urine; supersedes the stall-mat prescription (mats now optional comfort only).
7. **House door:** sealed (complete, pre-existing).

## Odor Doctrine (updated)
Original risk analysis stands: shared structure with the house is the core exposure. Mitigation now built into spec: negative-pressure exhaust (system 1), sealed house door (7), sealed slab (6), pens on the far wall from the house door. Remaining operational practice: ammonia absorbents under bedding, daily waste removal rhythm (volume estimate still open).
