## Medical

- 7+ day supply of all prescription meds, everyone
- First-aid kit
- EpiPens/inhalers if applicable
- Glasses/contacts spares
- If baby has arrived: OB/newborn records travel with the bag, not left for last

---

## Per-person bag (x8, scale down for the baby)

- 3 days clothing, sturdy shoes, jacket
- Water (1 gal/person/day target) and shelf-stable food
- Flashlight/headlamp + batteries
- Phone charger / battery pack

## Kid-specific

- Cullen, Emmitt, Rileigh — comfort item, diapers/pull-ups if in use, no-prep snacks
- Wyatt — owns and packs his own bag

## Pets

- Dogs: leash, food, bowls, vet records, current photo for ID if separated

## Livestock

- **Stockyard-owned, gated.** Coop/flock evacuation or shelter-in-place plan not logged here or in any Stockyard file until the durability gate clears. Talk through it live with Stockyard/Al when needed; nothing written to repo.

## Vehicles

- Keep tanks above half during fire season, standing rule
- Know in advance which two vehicles carry which people/animals if the household splits
