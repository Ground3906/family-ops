## Legal framework

**Colorado Graduated Driver License (GDL) program.** Three-stage: permit → restricted license → unrestricted license.

**HB24-1021** (effective Aug 2024) tightened restrictions on the restricted-license phase:
- Passenger restriction extended from 6 to **12 months** — no passengers under 21 in the vehicle other than immediate family during the first 12 months of licensure.
- Night driving restriction maintained — no driving between midnight and 0500 during the first 12 months, except for documented work/school/medical purposes.
- Wireless device prohibition during all restricted-license driving except for emergency calls.

**Driver education requirement (under 16):** to obtain a permit before age 16, applicant must complete an approved 30-hour classroom course **plus** 6 hours behind-the-wheel instruction from a licensed CO driver-ed provider. Certificate valid for **6 months** from date of completion.
